## 07. Motion specification, feel and tools
Desired feel: Apple-grade restraint, not sci-fi HUD animation. Smooth, grounded, responsive, interruptible. This is a quality target, not a brand-copy instruction. Use native CSS/Web Animations API, Motion for React/Framer Motion only if already installed or verified compatible, or GSAP only for tightly justified orchestration. Avoid mixing 3 competing animation runtimes.
Microinteractions: 140–220ms for hover/press/state, spring settling without cartoon bounce. State transitions: 220–420ms. Story bridge: 450–900ms per user-triggered step, with explicit focus and explanatory text. Landing entrance under ~900ms, never blocks interactions. Prefer transform/opacity; avoid layout thrashing, moving text baselines, continuously animated blur and forced reflow. Never make essential content depend on animation finishing. Use `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and visible static full story. No flashing, auto-play audio, perpetual neon pulses, floating doodles or parallax mountains. Limit one ambient movement on the entire page at a time, and pause when out of view. Target measured 60fps on representative machines; collect performance trace, not guess. Keep WebGL out unless demonstrably adds meaningful information without harming power and fallback.
